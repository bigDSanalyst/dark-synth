# A1 — x-Degree Grading

**Claim.** Theorem 3.6
**Failure mode.** FM4 (residual case) + FM1 (homonym trap)
**Bug.** Prior draft claimed `C_ℓ` is homogeneous of degree `ℓ` in `τ`.
**Caught by.** Counterexample `F = z₁⁵ + z₁ z₂` (degree 5 in z₁, degree 2 in z₁z₂ — a decoy higher-degree monomial).
**Fix.** Regrade by x-degree, not τ-degree. The strata are homogeneous of exact x-degree ℓ; τ-degree is irrelevant.
**Mutation test.** `[hand-audit-only]` — general statement over all F cannot be falsified by mutating k=2, m=2.
